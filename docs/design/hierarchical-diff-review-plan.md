# Hierarchical constitutional diff review — implementation plan

Status: proposed, 2026-09-22. This plan extends the [mixed-content model](https://linear.app/verfassung/document/mixed-content-constitution-structure-creation-and-editor-394a359fa467) and the [exact amendment-link plan](https://linear.app/verfassung/document/exact-constitutional-node-links-in-change-records-implementation-plan-026a9660a9f2). It depends on stable logical unit identities, immutable revisions, ordered text/child entries, version-aware occurrence resolution, and draft-to-published mapping from Sprints 40–44.

## Goal and current gap

When an editor compares a source snapshot with a draft or another pinned snapshot, the system should find differences at the unit that owns each change, then guide the editor through every finding. An amendment remains a curated, reviewed record; the engine supplies candidates and cannot decide whether a wording difference is legally substantive.

Today `AmendmentDiff.kt` flattens article/child trees, pairs by predecessor or kind plus number/label, and returns one node ID and a generated note. The gateway's “Fill from two versions” path then keeps only article, type, and note. `text-diff.ts` supplies visual word differences for public comparison but has no stable review-item identity. These paths cannot reliably review ordered parent text, moves, split/merge lineage, or a complete queue with saved progress.

## What counts as one difference

Normalize each side to a resolved, version-aware tree: top-level node, child nodes, and ordered content entries. Each node and text entry carries its logical identity, immutable revision identity, version-specific occurrence/reference, owner path, kind, literal label, title, and position. The target may be an in-memory projection of an editor draft; comparing it must not require saving a full constitution copy.

| Level/facet | Candidate example | Review behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Constitution/root or top-level structure | A chapter or article is inserted or removed | One candidate at the subtree root, with an expandable descendant preview. Do not count every descendant again by default. Derive level names from the version-pinned outline. |
| Node metadata | A paragraph's literal label or a sentence's title changes | Separate candidate for each changed field on its owning node. |
| Ordered content | A child or parent text entry moves before another entry | One move/reorder candidate with old and new parent/position. Do not misreport the move as remove plus add. |
| Text entry or explicit sentence node | Wording changes within an entry | One candidate for that entry. Word-level highlights are a presentation of this candidate, not extra review items. Never infer legal sentence nodes from punctuation. |
| Split/merge or uncertain lineage | One text entry becomes two, or two become one | Group the affected source and target refs; require an explicit pairing decision. Do not force a one-to-many case into an invented one-to-one link. |

## Top-down depth-first algorithm

Start at a synthetic constitution root so the first step also works when the configured top-level kind is not `article`. Build version-aware indexes of logical identities and explicit lineage before walking, then traverse paired trees depth first:

1. **Pair at the current level.** Match old and new nodes or entries by stable logical identity and explicit predecessor lineage. A changed literal label or position must not break a valid pair. If the same immutable *subtree* revision is reused, its contents are unchanged and the walker can skip descending, although a changed parent or order may still produce a move finding.
2. **Inspect only what this level owns.** Compare its kind, literal label, title, own text entries, and ordered direct child/text-entry sequence. A copy-on-write ancestor revision may differ solely because a descendant changed; do not emit an ancestor text change just because its revision ID changed. Align siblings by identity/lineage and relative order, so inserting one entry does not make every later sibling appear moved.
3. **Classify unmatched children before recursing.** Check the identity indexes for a counterpart under another parent: that is a move with old/new paths, not an unrelated deletion and addition. A genuinely added or removed subtree is one finding at its root; show its descendants in the preview without automatically making each one another task. Split/merge lineage becomes one review group with all affected refs. If pairing is ambiguous, flag that group and wait for an editor's mapping instead of guessing from labels or positions.
4. **Descend into each confirmed pair.** Repeat the same comparison for direct children and text entries until reaching the unit that actually owns the wording or metadata change. Run word-level diff only inside a paired text entry or explicit sentence node whose text changed. Never infer legal sentence nodes from punctuation.
5. **Roll findings up for navigation.** Emit actionable findings only for the owning unit or structural operation. Ancestors display counts of unresolved descendants, not duplicate findings. Use deterministic depth-first document order for the review queue. A resolved ambiguous group may reveal more individual findings; recompute the total and explain the new count in the progress tracker.

For example, a one-word edit in Article 8 → Paragraph 2 → Sentence 1 changes the copy-on-write revisions along that path. The walker pairs Article 8 and Paragraph 2, sees no change to their own fields, then emits one wording finding at Sentence 1. If Article 8 itself is removed, it emits one subtree-removal finding and stops descending that branch. A changed Article 8 title and a changed child sentence are two distinct findings at their respective levels.

Kind/label/position may rank *suggestions*, but cannot establish an authoritative pair. Draft review (ED-9) and amendment suggestions must use these same candidate rules.

Show a separate settings-impact notice if the two versions pin different structural settings revisions. Outline migration is reviewed through the constitution-settings workflow; a display-only settings change is not counted as a constitutional text difference. Version labels and dates are catalog facts, not text-entry diffs.

Use a pure Kotlin tree-diff module shared by editor-service and amendment-service, with adapters for editor draft projections and content-service snapshot reads. The module owns no database or HTTP writes. Content-service remains the authority for resolving a ref within a pinned version; editor-service owns edit-session decisions; amendment-service owns revisioned amendment rows. The gateway only renders server candidates and local word highlights. No service reads another service's database.

## Candidate and review contract

Return a deterministic document-order list of `DiffItem`s, each with a stable candidate key (logical unit or lineage group plus facet), source/target context IDs, `beforeRefs[]` and `afterRefs[]` (`node` or `text_entry`), old/new paths and excerpts, kind (`changed`, `added`, `removed`, `moved`, `metadata`, `split_merge`), level, confidence/ambiguity, and a content fingerprint. References always include version or draft context; a shared physical revision ID alone is not a link. For ordinary changes, the arrays contain the single before/after pair already planned for AMD-12. Split/merge needs a grouped reviewed relationship or an explicit extension of amendment rows; publication must not silently drop extra refs.

An editor decision is `open`, `linked`, or `excluded_with_reason`; a changed fingerprint or version pin makes the prior decision `needs_recheck`. `linked` points to one or more curated amendment rows, and multiple findings may be covered by one row. Exclusion requires a reason and reviewer acknowledgement; a substantive legal-text difference cannot disappear merely because a user hides it. If it is an editorial correction, direct the editor to the separate editorial session instead of turning it into a legal amendment. Keep the per-change public comment on the amendment row distinct from the internal exclusion reason.

Persist candidate decisions against a diff run scoped by source snapshot, target draft revision or snapshot, pinned structural settings revision, and algorithm version. Recompute after draft edits. Keep a decision only when its candidate key and fingerprint still match; mark affected findings for recheck otherwise. A source/target pin change invalidates the run. Saving and reopening the draft restores decisions and the current place in the queue. On legal publish, map draft target refs to published occurrences, recompute or validate against the final immutable pair, and refuse success if links or decisions are stale. For an existing snapshot pair, amendment-service persists the same decision shape on the draft change-record revision.

## Guided editor and progress tracker

The amendment editor has a **Review differences** queue alongside its existing change records. It shows `Reviewed 7 of 11`, counts for linked, excluded, open, ambiguous, and needs recheck, plus a progress bar with a text equivalent. The denominator is actionable findings only; parent roll-ups and word-highlight spans do not inflate it. Each outline-level navigation row shows a roll-up such as `Paragraphs: 3 open` without adding to the global total. A zero-diff result says “No differences found” and records the compared version pins.

The active panel shows `Difference 4 of 11`, its level and path, complete before/after unit text with word highlights, nearby parent/child context, exact refs, and any ambiguity. **Previous**, **Next**, and **Next unreviewed** follow stable document order. Filters by level, kind, status, and top-level unit narrow the queue while the global total stays visible; a filtered count is labeled separately. Clicking a finding in the constitutional canvas opens the same panel. Actions are **Link to change record** (new or existing row), **Correct pairing**, and **Exclude with reason** when permitted. After a decision, advance to the next unresolved finding; a reviewed finding stays reopenable. Keyboard focus, accessible labels, and non-color status text must work throughout.

The progress tracker is attached to the saved review run, not browser memory. A draft edit that invalidates three decisions removes them from the reviewed count and visibly changes progress, for example `Reviewed 4 of 11 · 3 need recheck`. Review and publication are blocked while open, ambiguous, or stale findings remain. The reviewer sees exclusions and their reasons before approval. This does not replace the existing reviewer/publisher and MFA gates.

## Delivery sequence

1. **Sprint 45 — contract and engine:** [PLAT-8](https://linear.app/verfassung/issue/VER-229/plat-8-canonical-hierarchical-diff-engine) defines fixture-based `DiffTree`/`DiffItem` contracts and the top-down depth-first algorithm, including sibling alignment, cross-parent moves, subtree pruning, and ambiguous lineage. Reuse ED-9's draft projection and PLAT-7's ordered traversal; [AMD-13](https://linear.app/verfassung/issue/VER-231/amd-13-paired-diff-suggestions-and-snapshot-review-state) upgrades `POST /amendments/suggest` to return paired, version-aware candidates without persisting suggestions. Keep the old response shape readable during gateway migration.
2. **Sprint 45 — review state:** [ED-11](https://linear.app/verfassung/issue/VER-230/ed-11-persist-draft-diff-review-decisions-and-progress) adds forward-only migrations and APIs for edit-session diff runs/decisions; AMD-13 adds revisioned decisions for a standalone amendment draft comparing existing snapshots. Validate fingerprint, pins, reviewer acknowledgement, and exact-ref mapping server-side. Preserve legacy amendment rows as readable but unresolved; do not fabricate old/new refs.
3. **Sprint 46 — gateway workflow:** [UI-66](https://linear.app/verfassung/issue/VER-232/ui-66-guided-amendment-diff-review-and-progress-tracker) replaces bulk “Fill from two versions” as the primary path with the one-at-a-time review queue and progress tracker. Keep the existing button as a way to start a comparison. Integrate the focused amendment mock's per-change comment and exact unit picker. The public compare view may reuse word rendering but does not own review decisions.
4. **Sprint 46 — verification and rollout:** [QLT-12](https://linear.app/verfassung/issue/VER-233/qlt-12-verify-hierarchical-diff-review-end-to-end) compares identical snapshots, one-word changes, nested parent text before/between/after children, custom outline levels, node metadata, moves, subtree add/remove, split/merge, duplicate labels, shared revisions, large trees, and editorial tip changes. Test save/reopen, source change, draft edit invalidation, keyboard flow, and legal publish retry. Benchmark on a large constitution; use subtree revision hashes to skip unchanged branches and page/render the queue without truncating the diff.

Acceptance: an editor can open two pinned versions or a legal draft, see every actionable difference exactly once at its owning level, review them sequentially, link each to exact old/new units or record a reviewed exclusion, leave and resume with accurate progress, and publish only when the final pinned pair and all decisions validate. Public amendment records remain curated rather than auto-generated.
