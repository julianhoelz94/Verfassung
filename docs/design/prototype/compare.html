Art. 20a · Protection of the natural foundations of life and animals

1949 not present

This article did not exist in the 1949 text.

2022 added 1994 · changed 2002

Mindful also of its responsibility towards future generations, the state shall protect the natural foundations of life and animals by legislation and, in accordance with law and justice, by executive and judicial action, all within the framework of the constitutional order.

Art. 3 · Equality before the law

1949
(2)

Men and women shall have equal rights.

(3)

No person shall be favoured or disfavoured because of sex, parentage, race, language, homeland and origin, faith, or religious or political opinions.

2022 changed 1994
(2)

Men and women shall have equal rights. The state shall promote the actual implementation of equal rights for women and men and take steps to eliminate disadvantages that now exist.

(3)

No person shall be favoured or disfavoured because of sex, parentage, race, language, homeland and origin, faith, or religious or political opinions. No person shall be disfavoured because of disability.
