# Read and compare constitutions

**Start here:** Countries → country → constitution → version. Public pages show published content. If sign-in is required, use the [[account guide|Account-Guide]].

## Read a text

1. Open **Countries** and select a country.
2. Choose its constitution and the legal version you need.
3. Follow the table of contents to a provision. Open its heading or choose **Full text** to show the wording.
4. Check its effective date and status.

## Text corrections and legal history

Reading pages emphasize legal versions, effective dates and legal changes. Routine typo and transcription fixes do not add another legal version to the version choices or a legal-change event to the timeline. Editorial details are secondary: staff can open **Structure and editorial details → Text corrections**, or use **Editorial → Snapshot history**, to inspect them. Correction entries remain collapsed until requested.

If you deliberately open a saved older text snapshot, a compact archive notice identifies it and offers **Read current text**. The older link continues to identify the same published text. Article history shows legal versions rather than a feed of typo corrections. On document pages, revision information is available under **Document details and revisions**.

## Check a text's provenance

Open **About** for the site's source labels and verification policy. A version or item marked **Official** has been checked against a cited official source; **Imported** means it came from a corpus and has not been fully checked. Inspect the cited source and version date before relying on a passage. The site is a research aid and does not replace the official publication or legal advice.

## Share or revisit a passage

Use the address of the opened version or unit as a permalink. Save the version ID with a citation, because a country's latest version can change later. The page for an older published snapshot remains distinct from the current one.

## Language and content translation

Use [[Account → Website language|Account-Guide#choose-a-language-or-translate-content]] to change the interface language or request content translation. Authored texts and machine translations are distinct; always check the source for exact wording.

## Site information and privacy

Use **About** for source and verification policy, **Impressum** for the published operator details, and **Datenschutzerklärung** for the published privacy notice. On a first visit, the informational necessary-cookie notice can be dismissed and links to the legal pages. The editorial and account functions require sign-in; published texts are available to public readers when anonymous browsing is allowed.

## If something looks missing

- Check that you selected the intended country, constitution, and version.
- Search covers published content; work waiting for review is not public.
- If a search result does not yet reflect a very recent publication, open the version page directly and try search again after indexing completes.
